# BagSections

**One combined bag, split into your own sections.** Keep your hearthstone, tools and
weapon swaps in their own spots, and let everything else land in "Rest". Each section
sorts on its own, so the things you always carry stay put.

## Features

- **Your own sections:** create, name, colour and reorder as many as you like.
- **Drag and drop:** drop an item on a section to add it, or drop it on Rest to take it out.
  Places you can drop light up blue, like Blizzard's own bags.
- **Sorts per section:** uses Blizzard's sort, and every section stays sorted on its own.
- **Smart matching:** add "every Hearthstone" or just "this exact sword".
- **Automatic Quest Items section** (optional).
- **Three layouts:**
  - **Default:** each section stacked at full width.
  - **Semi-compact:** sections side by side in rows. Unlock it and drag sections to build
    the rows you want.
  - **Compact:** one tight grid like the combined bag, with each section outlined in its
    colour.
- **Profiles:** save your section setup, load it on any character, or share it with a
  copy-and-paste code.
- **Looks at home:** Blizzard's bronze border and background, or a plain dark style, with
  adjustable opacity.
- **Collapse** any section, search, and see gold and free slots at a glance.

## Getting started

1. Press **B**. BagSections replaces the default bags.
2. Click the **gear** icon and choose **New section…**
3. Drag items from **Rest** onto your new section.

Alt + Right-click any item for quick options. Right-click a section name to rename,
recolour or move it. Type `/bs` to see the slash commands.

## Good to know

- Sections are saved per character. Profiles are shared across your account.
- Items aren't moved around in your real bags. Sections only change how the bag window
  shows them, so turning the addon off is always safe.
- Made for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.
